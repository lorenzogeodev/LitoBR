about=LitoBR downloads public geological data of Brazil straight into QGIS: lithostratigraphy at several scales, structures, mineral resources, outcrops, wells (SIAGAS), aquifers, geosites and caves from the SGB/CPRM ArcGIS REST services, state maps and mineral provinces from the RIGeo repository, and the 1:250,000 geology of BDIA/IBGE. Layers are clipped to the map canvas or to a mask, reprojected, styled with the official colour palette published by the data provider, and tagged with scale, year and an ABNT NBR 6023 citation in the layer metadata.
    Requirements and restrictions: internet connection; data cover Brazil only; no external Python libraries; the interface is in Brazilian Portuguese.
    Independent project: not affiliated with or endorsed by SGB/CPRM or IBGE. Data belong to their providers and follow their terms of use.
    PT-BR: baixa dados geológicos públicos do Brasil (SGB/CPRM, RIGeo e BDIA/IBGE) direto no QGIS, recorta pela tela ou por máscara, aplica a paleta oficial e grava escala, ano e a referência ABNT em cada camada. Projeto independente, sem vínculo com o SGB/CPRM ou o IBGE.
