# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First public release.

- SGB/CPRM ArcGIS REST services, RIGeo packages (26 states and 16 mineral provinces) and BDIA/IBGE.
- Clip to the map canvas, to a mask layer, or no clip.
- Official SGB colour palette (5,385 units), with a fallback palette by lithology and age.
- Recommendation of the most detailed product for the current map area.
- Scale, year and ABNT NBR 6023 citation stored in the layer metadata.
- Written for QGIS 3.22+ and QGIS 4 (Qt6).
